### Description

In this task, the feature matches obtained from Task 6 are analyzed to identify incorrect or outlier matches.
 RANSAC (Random Sample Consensus) is applied to estimate the correct geometric relationship between the two images 
while ignoring unreliable matches. The matches are visualized before and after applying RANSAC to clearly show the removal of outliers
RANSAC is useful because feature matching can produce incorrect correspondences, and removing these outliers improves the accuracy 
reliability of the matching process.
